The Destination

ONE ISLAND. TWO WORLDS.
ZERO COMPROMISES.

Zanzibar offers something few destinations in the world can: pristine Indian Ocean beaches paired with direct access to Africa’s greatest natural wonders.

Within a short flight, you can witness the Great Migration in the Serengeti, stand beneath Mount Kilimanjaro, or explore the rich history of Stone Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

EXPONENTIAL GROWTH.
LIMITED SUPPLY.

The fundamentals are equally compelling. Tourism has grown from 260,000 visitors in 2020 to more than 917,000 in 2025. New direct routes are opening. International attention is accelerating. Prime beachfront supply is scarce and finite.

Small wonder that Zanzibar has become one of the Indian Ocean’s most compelling emerging luxury property markets. The smart money is already here.

The Surrounds

BEYOND THE RESIDENCE.
A WORLD OF OPTIONS.

Zanzibar is an island where every direction offers a different experience. The Indian Ocean on one side. Safari adventures on the other. Ancient culture, untouched beaches, and modern luxury in perfect balance.

The Location

THE QUIETER SIDE OF ZANZIBAR’S
FINEST COASTLINE.

Bwejuu shares everything that makes this stretch of coastline internationally sought-after — the same turquoise lagoon, the same white sand, the same world-class kitesurfing conditions — without the pace and footfall of a major tourist destination. More private. More residential. Increasingly favoured by discerning buyers who know the difference.

You are not visiting Zanzibar.
You are owning it.

YOLO Residence sits 80 metres from the beach, connected by a direct beach path. Protected height restrictions on the properties directly ahead preserve uninterrupted Indian Ocean views from every residential floor.
